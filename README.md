# Fermor — Intelligent Capital Architecture

Fermor is an instrument-grade wealth and balance orchestration platform designed for high-conviction founders, family offices, and modern capital allocators. It transforms scattered financial silos into deliberate clarity, tactical leverage, and compound momentum.

---

## The Product Narrative

The homepage is structured as a continuous narrative rather than disconnected marketing blocks:

1. **Navigation**
   - Fermor wordmark & monogram
   - Real-time telemetry status badge (`SYSTEM: REAL-TIME CONVERGENCE`)
   - Direct section anchors (`Product`, `How it works`, `Action Engine`, `Intelligence`, `Trust & Security`)
   - Dual actions: `Sign In` and `Experience Fermor`

2. **Hero — Living Financial Canvas**
   - High-conviction editorial headline: *"Your money, finally making sense."*
   - Consolidated Net Balance readout (`$1,482,940.24`, `+$4,120.50 (24h)`)
   - Interactive SVG chart featuring historical trajectories, baseline inertia comparison, and real-time cursor coordinate tracking
   - 4-compartment asset allocation ribbon (Liquid Cash & Sweeps, Yield Equities & ETFs, Private Equity & LP, Real Asset Collateral) with interactive drilldown notes

3. **Chapter 01: The Anomaly — The Financial Problem**
   - Identifies the structural friction of legacy banks and fragmented logins
   - Institutional friction metrics (8.4 logins, 3.2% friction drag, $14,800 idle cash opportunity loss)
   - Interactive **Data Fragmentation Map & Shard Convergence Matrix**: users can toggle between scattered asset shards and the synchronized Fermor Axis

4. **Chapter 02: Revelation — Understand**
   - High-fidelity **Analytical Ledger Table** with 30D Velocity, 90D Rolling, and 1Y Macro filters
   - Status indicators (Optimized, Idle Drag, Rebalanced, Active LP) and real-time counterparty custodian data
   - **True Liquidity Ratio** card (4.8x coverage, surplus breakdown across Operating, Reserve, and Tax)
   - **Automated Savings Sweep** telemetry engine with toggle control

5. **Chapter 03: Decision Leverage — Act**
   - Tactical leverage detection alert (*"Fermor noticed an asymmetry in your cash reserves"*)
   - Interactive **Capital Sweep Simulator** with real-time range slider ($10k to $130k)
   - Dynamically calculates annual risk-free yield gain (+5.28% vs 0.05% checking)
   - Liquidity penalty indicator (0 business days auto-drawback guarantee)
   - Simulated order authorization flow with live state transition

6. **Chapter 04: Trajectory — Grow**
   - Interactive **Multi-Year Trajectory Model** with 3-Year, 5-Year, and 10-Year projection curves
   - Real-time SVG path morphing comparing Status Quo inertia against Fermor Orchestrated Execution
   - Compound delta metrics (+$1.28M Delta on 10Y, $184,200 yield drag eliminated, 120 cycles)
   - Goal progress gauges: Family Endowment Fund, Venture Angel Reserve, and Debt-Free Real Property

7. **Autonomous Observation — Intelligence Showcase**
   - *"Fermor noticed something."*
   - Real-world autonomous monitoring cards:
     - **Zombie Seats Creep**: Inactive SaaS subscription purge (+$740/mo recoverable)
     - **1099 Liquidity Event Drag**: Safe-harbor federal estimate and automated T-Bill escrow shielding
     - **Portfolio Drift Guard**: Concentration warning & collar hedging trigger
   - Interactive action resolution buttons

8. **Institutional Rigor & Security Proof — Trust**
   - Zero Payment for Order Flow (PFOF) mandate
   - $4.8B+ monitored assets & continuous SOC2 Type II compliance
   - Zero-Knowledge Hardware Vault Architecture with 256-bit AES encryption & read-only credential isolation

9. **The Resolution — Final Invitation**
   - Progressive sequence tracker: `01 UNDERSTAND → 02 ACT → 03 GROW`
   - Interactive Private Membership Allocation Console with tier selection (Sovereign Individual, Founder & Executive, Family Office)
   - Instant reservation feedback with allocated queue token

10. **Broadsheet Institutional Footer**
    - Multi-column site architecture, research monographs, and regulatory disclosures

## Technical Stack

- **Framework**: [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router, React 19, TypeScript)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) with a bespoke "Editorial Capital Craft" design system
- **Typography**: 
  - *Newsreader* (editorial headlines & monograph accents)
  - *Plus Jakarta Sans* (humanist interface body & controls)
  - *JetBrains Mono* (metric tabular numbers, coordinates, and telemetry badges)
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Animation & Interactivity**: Client-side SVG geometry morphing, cursor tracking, dynamic sliders, and reactive states.
